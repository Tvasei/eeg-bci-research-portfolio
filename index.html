Hardware design · Published in IET, 2024

A wearable motor-imagery decoder

EEG arrives slowly enough for several channels to share one digital filter. For my master’s research, I used that interval to reduce the circuitry needed to filter, extract features, and classify two imagined movements.

Paired EEG recordings · Research manuscript

From gel to dry electrodes

I compared a gel system and a dry headset on the same 35 participants. Their resting EEG spectra had similar shapes, yet a classifier could identify the recording device with an AUC of 0.999.

For past-month suicidal ideation classification, gel-to-dry AUC rose from 0.549 to 0.742 with asymmetric ComBat–CORAL alignment. In the reverse direction, the AUC change was −0.078. Paired 95% intervals for both changes included zero.

Doctoral research · Manuscript under review

Learning across six EEG cohorts

I trained a conditional variational autoencoder (CVAE) to reconstruct EEG under a common recording condition, then used a GATv2 graph classifier to distinguish depression from healthy controls. The full dataset contained 817 people and 32,987 EEG segments.

Held-out evaluation

What did the classifier retain?

Unharmonized EEG67.3%
Signal-level ComBat51.4%
CVAE reconstruction77.7%

Scores pool held-out predictions for 403 people in the two cohorts that included both depression and control participants.

What can I reproduce from this release?

Reproducing the study metrics requires the original training pipeline, the frozen CVAE, and the clinical recordings, which are outside this release.

Interpretability · Published in Algorithms, 2024

Reading brain responses to electroacupuncture

EEGNet decoded the stimulation phases of 48 participants receiving transcutaneous electroacupuncture (TEAS) with accuracies above 92%. I used saliency maps to ask where on the scalp the decision came from.

Seven electrodes — Fp1, Fp2, Fz, F7, F8, T3, T4 — carried the highest saliency, and a classifier retrained on those seven performed close to the full 19-electrode montage.
